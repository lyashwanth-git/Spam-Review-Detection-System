6. INTERPRETATION
- Exact duplicate text should not appear in both train and test partitions.
- A product-held-out split tests performance on products not seen during training.
- A category-held-out split is a harder test of transfer to unseen product categories.
- Strong metadata/label association is a warning to investigate source construction; it is not proof of bad labels.
- Rating and verified-purchase fields are NOT used as model input by the text-only classifier.
- Dataset labels are inherited from the source corpus and are not independently verified by this audit.
